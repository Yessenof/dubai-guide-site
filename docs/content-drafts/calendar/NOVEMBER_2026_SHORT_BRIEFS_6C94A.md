Attendance is open to trade and industry professionals. Big 5 spans the full DWTC complex across eight exhibition halls, the Za'abeel Halls, and the Sheikh Maktoum and Sheikh Rashid Halls.

## NOV-04-ADIPEC — ADIPEC 2026 (Abu Dhabi)

### EN brief (for brief_en field)

ADIPEC 2026 (Abu Dhabi International Petroleum Exhibition & Conference) takes place 2–5 November 2026 at ADNEC, Abu Dhabi. The event is the world's largest energy exhibition and conference, bringing together oil and gas companies, renewable energy firms, technology providers, government bodies, and investors from over 160 countries. The programme covers energy transition, decarbonisation, oil and gas operations, and upstream and downstream sectors. ADIPEC is in Abu Dhabi, approximately 130 km from Dubai.

Source: adipec.com — confirmed "2-5 November 2026, Abu Dhabi, UAE" on the official homepage (verified June 2026).
