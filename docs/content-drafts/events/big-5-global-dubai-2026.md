## What it is

Big 5 Global is the Middle East's leading annual trade exhibition for the building and construction sector. It takes place at Dubai World Trade Centre and spans the full venue complex across eight exhibition halls, the Za'abeel Halls, and the Sheikh Maktoum and Sheikh Rashid Halls.

The show covers structural building systems, interior fit-out and finishes, HVAC and MEP engineering, smart building technology, sustainable construction, and facility management. It is organised by DMG Events and is trade-only — attendees must be industry professionals.

---

## Who should pay attention

Big 5 Global is relevant if you:

- Work in construction, contracting, real estate development, or property investment in the UAE
- Run or are setting up a fit-out, contracting, or engineering company in Dubai
- Supply building materials, HVAC systems, smart home technology, or facility management services
- Are an architect or interior designer sourcing products and materials
- Are a property developer evaluating construction technology or sustainable building systems

## Planning notes

- Registration for Big 5 Global is required in advance. Check big5events.com for registration details.
- The event spans the full Dubai World Trade Centre complex — plan for multiple halls across 4 days.
- Dubai World Trade Centre is directly connected to the Dubai Metro (World Trade Centre station on the Red Line).
