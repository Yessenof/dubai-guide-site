## What it is

Dubai Design Week is the region's largest annual creative festival. It runs for six days across Dubai Design District (d3) and brings together design professionals, architects, interior specialists, brand buyers, and the general public.

The programme covers multiple strands: Downtown Design (a curated trade fair for contemporary and high-end design), public installations across d3, workshops, talks, exhibitions, and an open marketplace. The event runs under the patronage of HH Sheikha Latifa bint Mohammed bin Rashid Al Maktoum.

- Run or are setting up a design, creative, or branding company in Dubai
- Are a property developer or real estate investor with an interest in high-end interior trends
- Want to attend public design events and exhibitions in a premium district

## Downtown Design

Downtown Design is the curated fair component of Dubai Design Week, focused on contemporary and high-quality design products. It runs within the DDW week at d3. Exact 2026 dates for Downtown Design will be listed on the official programme at dubaidesignweek.ae closer to the event.

---

## Planning notes

- d3 (Dubai Design District) is located in the Al Quoz area, off Al Khail Road. It is not directly on a Metro line; take a taxi or rideshare.
- The full 2026 programme, individual event registration, and any ticketed components are published on the official website.
- Some exhibition halls and talks have limited capacity — check the official schedule in advance.
