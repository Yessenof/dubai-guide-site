## Why this matters for Guidex readers

The Abu Dhabi Grand Prix is one of the most high-profile events in the UAE calendar. It draws a large international audience and creates significant demand for hotels, flights, and transport across both Abu Dhabi and Dubai during the first week of December.

Early planning is important. Hotel availability in both cities tightens significantly in this period. Anyone planning a business-focused Dubai visit that includes Abu Dhabi should factor in the race weekend dates when setting their schedule.
