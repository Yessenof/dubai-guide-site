## Proposed title

How to Sponsor a Parent Residence Visa in Dubai

## Proposed summary

Sponsor a parent's UAE residence visa from Dubai. The sponsor must hold a valid UAE residence visa, meet income requirements noted by GDRFA, and provide attested relationship proof. Process runs through Amer service centres.

## Who this is for

UAE residents (employed, self-employed, or Golden Visa holders) who want to sponsor a parent's residence visa in Dubai.

---

## Overview (draft)

Para 1:
Parent sponsorship in Dubai runs entirely through Amer service centres under GDRFA residency rules. The sponsor does not need employer involvement -- the file is the sponsor's personal responsibility.

Para 2:
Total Amer-quoted package cost is typically AED 1,800–3,000 for the full file (entry permit, medical, Emirates ID, stamping), excluding any deposit. Processing takes approximately 3–6 weeks from entry permit to final stamping.

Para 3:
Documents -- sponsor provides: passport copy, physical Emirates ID (original), residence visa copy, signed Ejari tenancy contract (two-bedroom minimum per Amer notes), salary certificate or trade licence, 3-month bank statement, sponsor birth certificate attested by MOFA.

Para 4:
Documents -- applicant (parent) provides: passport (original), passport-size photo with white background, proof of relationship (sponsor birth certificate attested by country of origin, UAE Embassy, and UAE MOFA). Marriage or birth certificates must complete the full attestation chain before Amer will accept the file.

Para 5:
Amer filing notes: Amer service-centre notes reviewed by Guidex list an income reference of AED 10,500 for the sponsor, two-bedroom Ejari, and a refundable deposit of AED 5,000. A partner/investor file may reference AED 3,060 deposit. These figures reflect Amer's internal filing guidelines and are reviewed case by case at GDRFA -- they are not published statutory thresholds.

## Steps (draft)

### Step 1: Attest Relationship Proof
- **What:** Attest the sponsor's birth certificate through the correct chain: notarize in country of issue → authenticate at country foreign ministry → UAE Embassy stamp → UAE MOFA UAE attestation.
- **Where:** UAE MOFA (final step)
- **Advice:** Allow 2–4 weeks. Applications will be rejected at Amer without full attestation.

### Step 2: Confirm Income and Ejari Requirements
- **What:** Confirm sponsor income level and tenancy contract type before opening the file. Amer notes reference AED 10,500 salary and a two-bedroom Ejari.
- **Where:** Your Ejari center and employer/bank
- **Advice:** If the Ejari is for a studio or one-bedroom, check with Amer before applying -- upgrading mid-process causes delay.

### Step 3: Open Family File at Amer
- **What:** Submit full file at Amer: sponsor passport copy, original Emirates ID, residence visa copy, Ejari, salary proof, bank statement (3 months), parent passport, relationship attestation, photo.
- **Where:** Amer service center
- **Advice:** Bring sponsor physical Emirates ID. Copies are not accepted at this step.

### Step 4: Apply for Entry Permit
- **What:** Amer submits the entry permit application for the parent. The parent can enter inside (higher fee) or Amer applies for outside entry.
- **Where:** Amer service center
- **Fee note:** Amer notes: inside entry permit AED 1,089.90 / outside entry permit AED 439.90. These are Amer service-centre figures; final fee confirmed at counter.

### Step 5: Pay Deposit (if applicable)
- **What:** Amer may require a refundable deposit at the time of file processing. Deposit referenced in Amer notes: AED 5,000.
- **Where:** Amer service center
- **Advice:** Confirm deposit requirement and refund process with Amer before visiting. Deposit is returned on cancellation -- Amer notes confirm no deposit is required after cancellation, deposit of AED 2,000 before cancellation.

### Step 6: Complete Medical Fitness Test
- **What:** Parent attends an approved medical center for blood test and chest X-ray. Results submitted electronically to ICA.
- **Where:** GDRFA-approved medical center
- **Fee note:** Medical fee per Amer notes: AED 372.50
- **Advice:** Bring passport original and entry permit copy.

### Step 7: Apply for Emirates ID
- **What:** Biometric enrollment for Emirates ID at Amer.
- **Where:** Amer service center
- **Fee note:** 1-year EID: AED 286.50 / 2-year EID: AED 386.50

### Step 8: Finalize Residence Visa Stamping
- **What:** Final stamping of the parent's UAE residence visa in their passport.
- **Where:** Amer service center
- **Fee note:** 1-year stamping: AED 409.90 / 2-year stamping: AED 510.00
- **Advice:** Passport held briefly during stamping.

## Sponsor requirements (Amer filing notes)

- Physical Emirates ID (original)
- Passport copy
- Residence visa copy
- Salary proof: salary certificate (employed) or trade licence (self-employed)
- Ejari: two-bedroom minimum per Amer notes
- Bank statement: 3 months
- Sponsor income reference: AED 10,500 per month (Amer notes — reviewed case by case at GDRFA)
- Deposit: AED 5,000 refundable (Amer notes — confirm current requirement at counter)

## Applicant (parent) requirements

- Passport (original + copy)
- Passport-size photo (white background)
- Proof of relationship (sponsor birth certificate, fully attested by MOFA)
