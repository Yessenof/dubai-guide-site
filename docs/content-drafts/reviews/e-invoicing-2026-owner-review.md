## Additional note: list-format em dashes

The guide draft and news draft use the `**Bold term** — explanation` pattern in bullet lists (e.g., "What to review before the deadline", "What this guide does not cover"). This is a structured list format (term + definition), not prose. The em dash rule targets prose body copy — this pattern was not corrected. Owner may confirm whether this format is acceptable for published lists, or request a colon style (`**Bold term:** explanation`) as an alternative.

Two prose em dashes in the guide RU body were found and fixed after the initial scan: (1) SME deadline sentence restructured from `Срок выбора ASP — **31 марта**` to `Срок выбора ASP составляет **31 марта**`; (2) "annual revenue" definition sentence had `программы — облагаемый оборот` changed to `программы: облагаемый оборот`.
