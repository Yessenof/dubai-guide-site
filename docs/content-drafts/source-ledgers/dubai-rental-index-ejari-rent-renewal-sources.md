## Guidex angle

This is not generic real estate news.

Guidex should explain:

- **How tenants and owners can use official DLD tools before renewal.** The Rental Index calculator is an official service. Residents can use it before starting a renewal conversation with their landlord. Guidex explains what inputs the calculator needs and links to the official DLD page — it does not reproduce or predict the output.
- **Why rent renewal reminders should be tied to each tenancy contract date.** Unlike national holidays or compliance deadlines, rent reminders are personal. A calendar reminder set 90–120 days before the contract end date is more useful than a generic "rent season" post. Guidex Calendar should support this relative logic.
- **How Ejari connects to visas, DEWA, schools, banking, and family setup.** Ejari is not only a landlord/tenant document. Residents often need the Ejari certificate for visa renewals, DEWA account setup, school enrollment, and bank KYC. The Dubai Life Setup cluster should explain these connections.
- **How Rental Index and Smart Rental Index support planning but do not replace case-specific checks.** The Smart Rental Index explains the regulatory framework; the Rental Index calculator gives property-specific output. Neither replaces a conversation with a qualified tenancy adviser or legal professional for disputes.
- **Why Dubai Life Setup needs property reminders connected to the calendar.** Moving to Dubai involves multiple sequential steps — tenancy agreement, Ejari, DEWA, visa, school. A connected checklist tied to the move-in date is more useful than a standalone article.
- **When users should check official DLD/RERA tools or seek legal advice.** Guidex explains where to go. For questions about specific percentage increases, notice period disputes, or non-renewal conflicts, the guide always points to official DLD tools and qualified legal advice — not to Guidex content as the final answer.
