#### Facts captured from this source

- The Unemployment Insurance Scheme (ILOE) is an official UAE scheme with compliance obligations.
- MoHRE has officially communicated that fines will be imposed on workers who fail to comply.
- The scheme is employee-facing — workers are responsible for subscribing; do not overstate employer responsibility for worker ILOE subscription.
- Fine amounts referenced in MoHRE material must be rechecked against this source before any figure appears in published Guidex content.
- ILOE is relevant as a relative reminder after employment onboarding — timing is tied to work permit or employment activation, not a single fixed annual date.

**Note on this source:** This is a December 2023 MoHRE news item. Scheme rules, fine amounts, and compliance timelines may have been updated since. Do not publish fine amounts without rechecking against a current MoHRE source. A dedicated ILOE service/scheme page (not a news item) should be the primary source for a published guide.

## Guidex angle

This is not generic HR news. Guidex content in this area should be practical and specifically useful to the person starting a UAE business, hiring their first employee, or renewing a team member's residence permit.

**What a new UAE employer should understand after reading this content:**

- Which post-hiring obligations are employer-facing versus employee-facing
- How ILOE subscription relates to the work permit and employment visa process — and that the timing is relative, not a single annual deadline
- When and why basic health insurance becomes relevant — linked to permit issuance or renewal, not a general annual event
- How these topics connect: employment visa activation triggers ILOE and health insurance considerations
- Why Emiratisation is a separate track that only applies to companies above the threshold — and why it must not be conflated with general HR compliance topics

**How these topics connect to the Guidex content ecosystem:**

- Company setup guides: HR compliance is a post-licensing step — readers who set up a company will need this context as they hire
- Employment visa guides: ILOE and health insurance are natural add-ons after employment visa is activated
- Banking and payroll: WPS (Wage Protection System) is a related but separate MoHRE topic — not included in this ledger but worth noting for future planning
- Dubai Calendar: ILOE and health insurance reminders are relative, not fixed — they should appear as reminder types, not as regulatory deadline rows
- Emiratisation: high-risk, scoped to large private-sector employers — must be kept clearly separate from general HR content to avoid confusing small business owners

**What tone to avoid:**

- Do not write from the perspective of an HR law firm
- Do not make Guidex content read like a compliance checklist that claims completeness
- Do not overload the reader with every rule variant — explain the most common scenario, flag complexity, direct to professionals or MoHRE
- Do not make readers feel they are already non-compliant — frame these as things to check and verify, not accusations
