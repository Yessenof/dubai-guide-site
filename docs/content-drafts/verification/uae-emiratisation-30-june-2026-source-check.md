## The unresolved conflict

**Potential claim (not publishable yet):**
Private-sector companies with 50 or more employees may need to meet a semi-annual Emiratisation growth target before 30 June 2026, requiring 1% growth in UAE national employment in skilled jobs.

**Current status:** `source_signal_only`

**Publishing rule:** Do not publish any 30 June 2026 Emiratisation deadline or target figure until a stable official MoHRE source — a permalink, press release, service page, circular, or archived official document — is captured and recorded in the parent source ledger.

**Risk reason:** Emiratisation compliance is a high-risk topic. Incorrect scope, incorrect date, or incorrect target figures could mislead business owners about whether their company is in or out of scope, trigger unnecessary alarm, or understate actual obligations. A homepage snippet or transient banner is not sufficient for a published Guidex article.

## Source signal found

During Phase 6B and 6C research, the following signal was noted:

- MoHRE has publicly communicated Emiratisation obligations for private-sector companies with 50 or more employees.
- A 1% semi-annual growth target in UAE national skilled jobs has been referenced in MoHRE communications and official UAE government contexts.
- A 30 June 2026 deadline for the next semi-annual target check has appeared in research as an upcoming date for companies in scope.

**Why this signal is not sufficient for publish:**
- The signal originates from MoHRE homepage content or general awareness material — not a stable permalink or archived official circular.
- Homepage banners and current-affairs content can be updated, removed, or revised without notice.
- The specific wording of the obligation — which job categories count, how the 1% is calculated, what the base headcount is — has not been verified from a primary regulatory document.
- Penalty implications have not been captured from an official source.
- Scope boundary (exactly which company sizes, which sectors, which license types) has not been confirmed from a stable source.

**What makes this signal credible enough to plan with:**
- Emiratisation as a programme has been well-established in UAE federal policy since 2022.
- MoHRE has consistently issued semi-annual compliance communications.
- The 30 June date is consistent with the semi-annual cycle of prior years.
- This is useful for editorial planning — not for publication.
