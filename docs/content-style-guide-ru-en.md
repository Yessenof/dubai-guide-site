# Content Style Guide — EN + RU

Last updated: 2026-04-28

This guide defines writing standards for all Guidex Consulting content — English and Russian. It applies to guide fields (title, summary, overview, steps), hub pages, and landing pages.

The reference implementation is the `employment-visa` guide (April 2025 rewrite).

---

## Core principle

Write like a consultant who has done this process many times and wants to help someone get it right the first time. Not a journalist. Not a bureaucrat. Not an AI.

That means:
- State what happens and in what order
- Name the authority or service center handling each step
- Give real numbers: AED amounts, day counts, document lists
- Do not editorialize or build suspense

---

## Sentence structure

### Short declarative sentences only

Every sentence should carry one fact. If you need two clauses, split into two sentences.

**EN — bad:**
> Once you have gathered your documents — which include your passport, Emirates ID, visa copy, and medical certificate — you can proceed to the Amer Center, where an agent will review your application and, upon confirmation, submit it to the GDRFA on your behalf.

**EN — good:**
> Gather your passport, Emirates ID, visa copy, and medical certificate. Take them to any Amer Center. An agent reviews the file and submits it to GDRFA.

**RU — bad:**
> После того как вы собрали все необходимые документы — в их числе паспорт, Emirates ID, копия визы и медицинская справка — можно направляться в Amer Center, где сотрудник проверит вашу заявку и при положительном исходе передаст её в GDRFA от вашего имени.

**RU — good:**
> Подготовьте паспорт, Emirates ID, копию визы и медицинскую справку. Обратитесь в любой Amer Center. Сотрудник проверит документы и подаст заявку в GDRFA.

---

## Em dashes

Em dashes are banned as clause separators in all content fields.

They create run-on sentences and signal poor sentence structure. Replace with a period.

**EN — bad:**
> Submit the application — the agent will confirm receipt within 24 hours.

**EN — good:**
> Submit the application. The agent confirms receipt within 24 hours.

**RU — bad:**
> Подайте заявку — агент подтвердит получение в течение 24 часов.

**RU — good:**
> Подайте заявку. Агент подтвердит получение в течение 24 часов.

Exception: em dashes in parenthetical asides are allowed when genuinely required for readability (rare). When in doubt, use parentheses or a new sentence instead.

---

## Opening — costs and timelines first

Readers want to know: how much does this cost, and how long does it take? Give these in the first two paragraphs of the overview. Do not make them hunt for it.

**EN — bad:**
> Sponsoring your spouse for a Dubai residence visa is a multi-step process that requires coordination between several government authorities. It involves medical testing, Emirates ID registration, and GDRFA approval.

**EN — good:**
> Sponsoring a spouse on a Dubai residence visa takes 2–4 weeks end to end. Government fees are approximately AED 2,000–2,500 depending on visa duration. The process runs through the Amer Center and GDRFA.

**RU — bad:**
> Оформление резидентской визы для супруги в Дубае — многоэтапный процесс, требующий взаимодействия с несколькими государственными органами, включая медицинский осмотр, регистрацию Emirates ID и одобрение GDRFA.

**RU — good:**
> Виза для супруги в Дубае оформляется за 2–4 недели. Государственные сборы составляют около AED 2 000–2 500 в зависимости от срока визы. Процесс проходит через Amer Center и GDRFA.

## Theatrical framing — banned

These phrases add no information. Delete on sight.

| Banned phrase | Replace with |
|---|---|
| "This is the most critical step" | State why it matters, or omit |
| "This is where the real work begins" | Start the next sentence |
| "You are now ready to proceed" | Proceed |
| "Navigating Dubai bureaucracy can feel overwhelming" | Delete entirely |
| "The good news is…" | State the fact |
| "Fortunately…" | Delete |
| "This process may seem complex, but…" | Delete |
| "Don't worry —" | Delete |

**RU equivalents — also banned:**
- "Это ключевой шаг в процессе"
- "Именно здесь начинается настоящая работа"
- "Хорошая новость состоит в том, что…"
- "Не переживайте —"
- "Несмотря на кажущуюся сложность, процесс…"

---

## AI verbosity — banned

These phrases appear in AI-generated content and signal that the text was not written by someone who knows the process.

| Banned pattern | Notes |
|---|---|
| "seamlessly" | Delete |
| "crucial" / "critical" | Say why, or delete |
| "comprehensive" | Delete |
| "streamline" / "streamlined" | Delete |
| "navigate" (the process) | Say "complete" or "go through" |
| "leverage" | Use "use" |
| "ensure" (overused) | Use "confirm" or be specific |
| "in this guide, we will…" | Do not narrate the guide |
| "it is important to note that…" | Put the note in the relevant step |
| "please note that…" | Same |
| "as mentioned above" | Restructure so the note appears once |

**RU equivalents — also banned:**
- "данное руководство" (use "статья" or refer to specific steps)
- "необходимо отметить, что…"
- "как было упомянуто выше"
- "данный процесс может показаться сложным, однако…"
- "мы расскажем вам обо всём"

## Russian-specific writing rules

### Do not write translated English

Russian content must read as if written by a Russian-speaking professional, not translated from English.

**Translated-English feel — bad:**
> Спонсор должен обеспечить то, чтобы его доход соответствовал минимальным требованиям GDRFA.

**Natural Russian — good:**
> Зарплата спонсора должна соответствовать минимальному порогу GDRFA.

### Use Russian terminology where it exists

Use the Russian term when one exists naturally. Use the English/transliterated term when Russian professionals themselves use it.

| Concept | Use |
|---|---|
| Residence visa | резидентская виза (not "виза на проживание") |
| Employment visa | рабочая виза (or трудовая виза) |
| Free zone | фризона (transliterated, widely used) |
| Mainland | материковая часть / mainland (context-dependent) |
| Emirates ID | Emirates ID (not translated) |
| PRO services | PRO-услуги (transliteration standard) |
| GDRFA | GDRFA (not translated) |
| MOHRE | MOHRE (not translated) |
| Golden visa | золотая виза (translated — widely used) |
| Sponsor | спонсор |
| Dependent | зависимый / иждивенец |

### Fees in Russian content

Write AED amounts with a space between the number and AED. In RU content, the standard is:
- AED 2 500 (space-separated thousands, not a period)
- "около AED 2 000–2 500" (not "2,000–2,500")

### Formal but not bureaucratic

Russian content should feel like a knowledgeable colleague explaining a process, not a government document.

Use "вы" (lowercase) — not "Вы" (which reads as overly formal and dated).

## Titles

### English

Specific, searchable, direct. No "Ultimate Guide" framing.

- Good: "How to Get an Employment Visa in Dubai Without Leaving the UAE"
- Good: "Child Dependent Visa Dubai: Complete Process for Residents"
- Bad: "Everything You Need to Know About Dubai Visas"
- Bad: "The Complete Guide to Dubai Residence"

### Russian

Mirror the EN title intent, but write naturally in Russian — not word-for-word translation.

- EN: "How to Sponsor a Spouse Visa in Dubai"
- RU good: "Виза для супруги в Дубае: как оформить резидентство"
- RU bad: "Как спонсировать визу для супруга в Дубае" (sounds translated)

---

## Summary field (meta description)

- 1–2 sentences max
- Must work as a standalone meta description (Google shows ~155 chars)
- States: what the guide covers + which authority handles it + a notable specific (fee, timeline, or route)

**EN example:**
> Step-by-step guide to sponsoring a spouse residence visa in Dubai. Covers Amer Center submission, GDRFA approval, and Emirates ID registration. Government fees approximately AED 2,000–2,500.

**RU example:**
> Пошаговое руководство по оформлению визы для супруги в Дубае. Подача через Amer Center, одобрение GDRFA, регистрация Emirates ID. Государственные сборы — около AED 2 000–2 500.

---

## "Who this is for" field

- 1–2 sentences describing the exact reader
- Specific about visa status, employment type, or situation
- Not a vague category

**EN — bad:**
> People who want to bring family to Dubai.

**EN — good:**
> Dubai residents on a 2-year residence visa sponsored by a mainland employer, looking to add a spouse to their family file.

**RU — bad:**
> Люди, которые хотят перевезти семью в Дубай.

**RU — good:**
> Резиденты Дубая на 2-летней визе, спонсируемые материковым работодателем, которые хотят оформить визу для супруги.

---

## Overview field

- 2 short paragraphs max
- Para 1: what the route is, which authority handles it, what the reader's role is
- Para 2: total cost range, timeline, key step count
- Do not preview the steps — the steps section does that
- Do not use "In this guide" or "You will learn"

---

## Step fields

**Title:** Short, action-oriented, 3–6 words.
- Good: "Submit application at Amer Center"
- Bad: "Going to the Amer Center to Submit Your Application Documents"

**What:** 1–2 sentences. Describe the action, not the background.

**Where:** Authority or service center name only.

**Address/portal:** "Any [Name] branch in Dubai" or a specific portal URL. Never invent physical addresses.

**Cost:** Exact AED amount if confirmed. If variable: explain the variation. Do not write a range unless it is well-supported by official tariffs.

**Time:** Exact day count where possible.

**Advice:** Only when it adds real value the reader could not guess.

**Warning:** Only for genuine risk of error, delay, or money lost.

---

## Fee discipline

- Do not invent ranges
- Only retain ranges supported by official government tariffs or well-documented service center pricing
- When exact fee is unknown: name the drivers of variation instead
- Example: "Fee varies by visa duration and Emirates ID issuance charges. Amer confirms at submission."
- Well-supported range retained: medical fitness test AED 250–450 (GDRFA-approved center range)

---

## Mobile-first readability

- Short paragraphs: 2–3 sentences max in any prose block
- No bullet lists with more than 5 items without a subheading
- Steps must be self-contained — a reader can screenshot one step and execute it
- Do not require the reader to scroll back to understand the current step

---

## Checklist before publishing any guide

- [ ] No em dashes used as clause separators
- [ ] No theatrical framing
- [ ] No AI verbosity phrases
- [ ] Summary is ≤ 2 sentences
- [ ] Overview is ≤ 2 paragraphs
- [ ] Step "what" fields are ≤ 2 sentences each
- [ ] Costs given exactly where known; variation explained where not
- [ ] Timeline given in the overview (not buried in steps)
- [ ] No invented physical addresses
- [ ] RU text reads like a native speaker, not a translation
- [ ] Fee ranges have an identified source or are removed
